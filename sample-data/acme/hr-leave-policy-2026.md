# Acme Leave Policy (2026)

Policy ID: HR-LEAVE-2026
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Supersedes: HR-LEAVE-2024

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

## Carryover

Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next year.
Carried-over days expire on June 30.

## Parental leave

Employees who become parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave,
which can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note
is required for absences longer than 5 consecutive days.
