# Acme Procurement Policy

Policy ID: FIN-PROC-04
Effective date: 2025-09-01

## Purchase orders

A purchase order is required for any purchase above 1,000 USD.

## Competitive quotes

Purchases above 10,000 USD require at least three competitive quotes.

## Vendor onboarding

New vendors that handle customer data must pass a security review before
signing a contract.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms for vendors are net 30 days.
