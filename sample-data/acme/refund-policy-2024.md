# Acme Refund Policy (2024)

Policy ID: REF-2024-01
Effective date: 2024-01-01

## Eligibility

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. The purchase
date is the date shown on the order confirmation email.

## How to request a refund

Contact support with the order number. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 5 business days.

## Exclusions

Gift cards and downloadable content that has been accessed are not refundable.
