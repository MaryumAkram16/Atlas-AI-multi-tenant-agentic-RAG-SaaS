# Acme Refund Policy (2026)

Policy ID: REF-2026-07
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Supersedes: REF-2024-01

## Eligibility

Customers may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase. Requests after
7 days are declined unless an exception in another policy applies.

## How to request a refund

Submit the refund form in the customer portal with the order number. Refunds
are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.

## Exclusions

Gift cards, accessed downloadable content and custom orders are not refundable.
