# Acme Employee Benefits Policy

Policy ID: BEN-2025-01
Effective date: 2025-01-01

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for benefits after 30 days of employment.

## Health insurance

Acme pays 80 percent of health insurance premiums for employees and 50 percent
for dependants. Dental cover is included.

## Pension

Acme matches pension contributions up to 5 percent of base salary.

## Wellness stipend

Employees receive a wellness stipend of 50 USD per month for gym memberships
or fitness classes.
