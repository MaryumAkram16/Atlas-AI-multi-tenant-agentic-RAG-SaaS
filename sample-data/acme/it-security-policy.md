# Acme IT Security Policy

Policy ID: IT-SEC-05
Effective date: 2025-01-15

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Password managers are
recommended; reusing passwords across services is prohibited.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the VPN and all
customer-data systems.

## Incident reporting

Report suspected security incidents, including lost devices, to the security
team within 24 hours at security@acme.example.

## Device encryption

All laptops must use full-disk encryption.
