# Acme Holiday Calendar 2026

Policy ID: HR-HOL-2026
Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Public holidays

Acme observes 11 public holidays in 2026.

## Year-end closure

Offices are closed from December 24 to January 1. Closure days do not count
against annual leave.

## Floating holidays

Each employee may take 2 floating holidays per year for personal or religious
observances.
