# Acme Code of Conduct

Policy ID: HR-COC-01
Effective date: 2024-06-01

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts worth up to 100 USD per year from any single
business partner. Gifts above that value must be declined or reported.

## Conflicts of interest

Potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed to your manager within
14 days of becoming aware of them.

## Reporting concerns

Concerns can be reported anonymously through the ethics hotline at
ethics@acme.example. Acme does not tolerate retaliation against reporters.
