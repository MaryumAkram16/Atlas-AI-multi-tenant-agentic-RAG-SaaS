# Acme Remote Work Policy

Policy ID: HR-REMOTE-01
Effective date: 2025-03-01

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to
3 days per week, subject to manager agreement.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be available during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 local
office time.

## Equipment stipend

New remote workers receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of
400 USD. Company laptops remain company property.

## Working abroad

Working from another country requires HR approval and is limited to 20
working days per year.
