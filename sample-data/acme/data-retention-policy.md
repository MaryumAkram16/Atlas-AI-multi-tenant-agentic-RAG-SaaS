# Acme Data Retention Policy

Policy ID: IT-RET-02
Effective date: 2025-05-01

## Customer data

Customer account data is retained for 24 months after the account is closed,
then deleted.

## Application logs

Application logs are retained for 90 days.

## Deletion requests

Customer deletion requests are completed within 30 days of verification.

## Backups

Backups are kept for 35 days and are encrypted at rest.
