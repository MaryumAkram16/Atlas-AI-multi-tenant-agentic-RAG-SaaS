# Acme Travel and Expenses Policy

Policy ID: FIN-EXP-03
Effective date: 2025-07-01

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims with itemized receipts within 30 days of the expense.
Claims submitted later than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Meals

Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day.
Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Approvals

Any single expense above 500 USD requires written approval from your manager
before it is incurred. Expenses above 5,000 USD also require finance approval.

## Travel class

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy
is allowed for longer flights.
