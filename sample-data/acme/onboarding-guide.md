# Acme Onboarding Guide

Policy ID: HR-ONB-03
Effective date: 2025-04-01

## First day

New hires receive their laptop on the first day and are assigned an
onboarding buddy.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 90 days. Managers hold a review meeting at the end
of probation.

## Training

Mandatory security and compliance training must be completed within 30 days
of the start date.
